# Identify Your Guition JC8012P4A1 Display

The [installer](/install#choose-the-correct-panel-firmware) lists the firmware choices. Use these checks if you are unsure which one matches your display.

## Identify your revision

1. **Check the chip revision.** ESPHome startup logs may report it. If it is **ESP32-P4 v3.x**, choose **V3**, regardless of the case number.
2. **For other chip revisions, check the four-digit rear-case number.** Choose **V1** for `2627` or lower, or **V2** for `2628` or higher. The case may not say V1 or V2.

If you still cannot confirm the hardware, [ask for help](https://github.com/jtenniswood/espframe/issues) with a photo of the rear label and any chip information you have.

## Install

**[Return to the installer](/install#choose-the-correct-panel-firmware)** and select the matching hardware version. WiFi and Immich setup are the same for every supported display.
