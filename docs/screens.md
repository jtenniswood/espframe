# Where to Buy

EspFrame supports the **10.1-inch Guition JC8012P4A1**: an ESP32-P4 touchscreen with a 1280 × 800 landscape display. Other display models are not currently supported by the ready-to-install firmware.

<span id="buy-and-mount-the-display"></span>

| What you need | Link |
| --- | --- |
| Guition JC8012P4A1 display | [Buy on AliExpress](https://s.click.aliexpress.com/e/_c4LLo3rH) |
| Printable stand | [Download from MakerWorld](https://makerworld.com/en/models/2490049-guition-p4-10inch-screen-stand#profileId-2736046) |

Confirm the model before ordering. The [installation guide](/install) helps you choose the matching firmware and walks you through setup.
